How points and weights work

KPI Weight in Environment (%) totals 100% when the environment has points. When it has 0 points, saved relative weights remain editable and are used when points are assigned. Global weight (%) shows the KPI’s share of all environments. Setting an environment total redistributes its KPIs by their relative weights; editing its global weight preserves total points and redistributes other environments proportionally.
